Privacy in this version

How this version of the website handles information.

Preparing your project brief

The contact fields prepare a message in your browser. This version does not save the fields to a database or send them to the website server. Opening the email draft uses your email application; you decide whether to send it. The copy button copies the message to your clipboard.

Contact links and external services

WhatsApp, email and phone links open the selected service or application, where its provider’s policies apply. This version has no active payment gateway or customer accounts.

Hosting and measurement

This version’s code includes no marketing trackers or local storage of form data. The hosting provider may process technical information required to serve the site, such as network addresses and request logs. This page should be reviewed when new services are added.

Contact

For questions about information you send to Thabot, contact info@thabot.com. Do not enter bank information, passwords or confidential material in the project brief.
